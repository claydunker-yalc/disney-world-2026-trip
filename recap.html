Tuesday, June 2
Arrival + Animal Kingdom Lodge
Kidani / Jambo / Sanaa

The trip landed cleanly: Michael met, pickup done, Animal Kingdom Lodge by about 1:30, then straight into the first resort-food-and-room-logistics chapter.

HotelRoom 7977

The room was insanely far from the elevators — cart-pushing past 7901 through 7976 — but the tradeoff was excellent: booked Standard View, received Savanna View.

LunchThe Mara

Poppy crushed the three-cheese flatbread and called it “so good.” Rosie went chicken strips with double apple slices and apple juice. Clay and Kristin shared jerk chicken strips with pineapple salsa.

1:30-ish

Arrival

Animal Kingdom Lodge arrival after pickup, then a walk over to Jambo House for quick service.

3:00 PM

Walmart delivery

Groceries were scheduled for the room-arrival window.

Afternoon

Pool + splash pad

Swimming and splash pad time filled the space before the Sanaa reservation.

DinnerSanaa report card

  • Bread service: famous for a reason. Clay’s favorite sauce was pickled garlic; Kristin leaned chimichurri or hummus; Clay also loved the hummus.
  • Kristin’s flat iron steak was only okay — a little too much fat and gristle, and not seasoned well enough.
  • Poppy’s butter chicken with mac and cheese was a 9.5/10 and immediately became one of her favorites.
  • Rosie had kids’ grilled chicken with tomato soup; the soup did not impress.
  • Clay’s braised beef with red beans / rajma masala and jasmine rice was delicious.
  • Drinks: Rosie water, Kristin African Mai Tai, Clay Bee’s Knees, Poppy watermelon lemonade.
  • Service was underwhelming; waitress was Lindsay.
Wednesday, June 3
Animal Kingdom: the full campaign
82° and cloudy

Everyone fell asleep around 8:30 Eastern, then the girls were up before 6 and ready to go. The plan was Animal Kingdom rope drop, and the day became a greatest-hits reel with snacks, pins, rider swap bravery, and a spectacularly soggy finale.

RidePoppy’s thrill résumé

Flight of Passage twice. Expedition Everest twice. After Everest round one: “I think I like it.” During the climb on round two: “Why am I doing this again?” Final verdict: forward hills yes, backward hills in the dark maybe not, champ status confirmed.

LogisticsBlue-tent diplomacy

Kristin worked with the blue-tent cast members to get Lightning Lane plans merged with Courtney and her family for the next day — vacation logistics as treaty negotiation.

SafariKilimanjaro animal haul

The Safari delivered a first-time mandrill sighting plus three lions — one male and two lionesses — flamingos, both white and black rhinos, cheetahs, hippos, and ostriches. Poppy’s favorites were the lions; Rosie’s were the flamingos.

Rope drop

Flight of Passage

Completed early, with Poppy getting a bonus second ride. Bathroom breaks and popcorn break survived.

Morning

Safari + pin economy

Kilimanjaro Safaris, lots of pin trading, cookie dough brownie ice cream sandwiches for Poppy and Kristin, a quick sit break, and a popcorn refill for later.

Late morning

Zootopia in the Tree of Life

A seated reset after the early sprint.

Midday

Mickey, Minnie, Everest

Clay handled stroller logistics while Kristin took the girls to meet Mickey and Minnie. Then Everest with rider swap gave Poppy her double run.

Lunch

Na’vi + Satu’li

Rosie earned an Everest gift shop scavenger-hunt badge, the family decompressed on Na’vi River Journey, then ate at Satu’li: cheeseburger pods, wrapped hot dog, beef/shrimp noodle bowl with green onion vinaigrette, margarita, and Clay’s mystery Captain Morgan/chamoy/orange juice drink.

Afternoon

MagicBand+ + Bluey

Rosie needed her own MagicBand+, then the family took the train to Conservation Station to see Bluey.

Evening

Kali + Nomad Lounge

Rosie loved Kali River Rapids and was the only one who wore her poncho. Afterward: Nomad Lounge patio couch booth, churros, Annapurna Zing for Kristin, Spice Trader Classic for Clay, and a fresh clean shirt because Clay thought ahead.

DinnerFlame Tree Barbecue

Kristin and the girls handled dinner while Clay did not really want anything. Kristin’s apple salad was delicious; Rosie got the pulled pork kids meal; Poppy went pulled pork, fries, and chicken leg/drumstick meal.

FinaleAlready wet, destiny chosen

The family ended the evening with Kali River Rapids again — twice. That turned the day from “park day” into an open-to-close family first.

“That was the first time all four of us together had opened and closed a park as a family — it was Animal Kingdom with shorter hours, but hey, we did it.”

ResortCommunity Hall side quest

Back at the resort, nobody was actually donezo: Community Hall, DIY Minnie ears for Poppy, a decorated tumbler for Rosie, and the end of the live-action Lilo & Stitch. Clay called it an amazing day.
