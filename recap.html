Tuesday, June 2
Arrival day, ranked by usefulness
Kidani / Jambo / Sanaa

The arrival day was mostly about getting in cleanly, finding the room, feeding everyone, and learning that “far from the elevator” can still come with a very good view.

LogisticsThe travel handoff worked

Michael met the family, pickup was done, and everyone made it to Animal Kingdom Lodge around 1:30. No big arrival drama. That counts.

Why it mattered: the trip started settled instead of scrambled.

HotelRoom 7977 was a hike, but the view won

The room was wildly far from the elevators. Clay pushed the cart past 7901 through 7976, which is basically a resort-side quest. The payoff: booked Standard View, got Savanna View.

Final ruling: annoying walk, excellent upgrade.

LunchThe Mara did its job

  • Poppy ate every bite of the three-cheese flatbread and called it “so good.”
  • Rosie went chicken strips, double apple slices, and apple juice.
  • Clay and Kristin split jerk chicken strips with pineapple salsa.
Best review: Poppy’s flatbread endorsement.

DinnerSanaa was mostly about the bread service

  • Bread service was famous for a reason. Clay’s favorite sauce was pickled garlic; Kristin leaned chimichurri or hummus.
  • Poppy’s butter chicken with mac and cheese got a 9.5/10.
  • Clay’s braised beef with red beans / rajma masala and jasmine rice was delicious.
  • Kristin’s flat iron steak was only okay: too much fat and gristle, not enough seasoning.
  • Rosie’s tomato soup did not win anyone over.
Best bite: bread service. Biggest miss: service, which was underwhelming.
Wednesday, June 3
Animal Kingdom in quick hits
82° and cloudy

Animal Kingdom became the first all-four-family open-to-close park day. Shorter park hours, yes. Still counts. The day had thrill-ride bravery, actual good animal luck, pin trading, wet shoes, and one very useful clean shirt.

RidePoppy built a thrill-ride résumé fast

Flight of Passage twice. Expedition Everest twice. After Everest round one: “I think I like it.” During the second climb: “Why am I doing this again?”

Final ruling: forward hills yes, backward hills in the dark maybe not.

SafariThe safari actually delivered

Kilimanjaro Safaris had a first-time mandrill sighting, three lions, flamingos, white and black rhinos, cheetahs, hippos, and ostriches.

Kid favorites: Poppy picked the lions; Rosie picked the flamingos.

LogisticsKristin handled the blue-tent treaty

Kristin got the Lightning Lane plans merged with Courtney and her family for the next day. Disney logistics, but with diplomacy.

Why it mattered: all nine people could move through the next park days together.

SnacksThe snack economy was strong

  • Lots of pin trading.
  • Cookie dough brownie ice cream sandwiches for Poppy and Kristin.
  • A popcorn refill saved for later.
  • Satu’li lunch: cheeseburger pods, wrapped hot dog, beef/shrimp noodle bowl, margarita, and Clay’s mystery Captain Morgan/chamoy/orange juice drink.
Most mysterious item: Clay’s unnamed drink.

ResetThe middle of the day had useful resets

Zootopia in the Tree of Life gave everyone a seated break. Na’vi River Journey gave everyone a boat-ride decompression. Rosie earned an Everest gift shop scavenger-hunt badge.

Best reset: Na’vi after Everest energy.

FinaleKali River Rapids became the day’s whole personality

Rosie loved Kali and was the only one who wore her poncho. Later, the family rode Kali again twice because everyone was already wet and destiny had apparently been chosen.

Best preparedness: Clay having a fresh clean shirt ready.

LoungeNomad Lounge was the adult recovery booth

After Kali, everyone landed on the Nomad Lounge patio couch booth for churros and drinks: Annapurna Zing for Kristin, Spice Trader Classic for Clay.

Best use case: pretending the wet chaos was actually part of the plan.

ResortThe night still had a resort side quest

Back at the resort, the family went to Community Hall. Poppy made DIY Minnie ears, Rosie decorated a tumbler, and everyone caught the end of the live-action Lilo & Stitch.

Clay’s review: an amazing day.

“That was the first time all four of us together had opened and closed a park as a family — it was Animal Kingdom with shorter hours, but hey, we did it.”

Thursday, June 4
Polynesian move day, no narrative required
Boma / Trader Sam’s / Poly lawn

June 4 was the resort-switch day: pack up, eat Boma, move to the Polynesian Tower, reset the room, reunite with cousins, and end on the lawn with pizza and Dole Whips.

Photo reel

June 4 Polynesian Tower

1 / 1

BreakfastBoma anchored the morning

The family woke up, packed up Animal Kingdom Lodge, and had Boma before the resort switch.

Best function: making move day feel less like pure logistics.

HotelThe Polynesian Tower reset worked

Everyone took a Lyft to the Polynesian Tower, checked in, and got pool time started. Clay went to Trader Sam’s at 1:30, then left around 2:30 to check out the room with Poppy and Rosie while Kristin stayed by the pool.

Best split: pool time plus room recon.

ResetThe room became a recovery zone

The girls watched Wreck-It Ralph in bed in their swimsuits while Clay handled grocery delivery and luggage delivery. Kristin finished her book and said the ending was worth the slow part.

Most vacation image: swimsuits in bed, movie on, adults solving logistics.

ReunionThe cousin reunion changed the energy

Courtney, Christian, Ray, Jude, and Sloane arrived not long after, and everyone was very excited to see each other.

Why it mattered: the trip shifted from family-of-four mode to big-group mode.

DinnerPizza, Dole Whips, and the Electrical Water Pageant closed it out

Everyone ordered pizza for a picnic at the Polynesian lawn area, followed by Dole Whips. The night ended with the Electrical Water Pageant before everyone headed back to their rooms.

Best simple plan: lawn pizza with a built-in Disney ending.
